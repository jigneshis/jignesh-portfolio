while a full web application can take 4-8 weeks. I'll provide a detailed timeline
estimate after our initial discussion about your project scope.
```

**Q2: What technologies do you work with?**
```
I specialize in the modern JavaScript/TypeScript ecosystem: React, Next.js, and
Node.js on the frontend and backend, with PostgreSQL, MongoDB, or Supabase for
databases. I also work with Redis, AWS cloud services, and Tailwind CSS for styling.
```

**Q3: Can you work with my existing codebase?**
```
Absolutely. I'm experienced in jumping into existing projects — whether it's adding
new features, fixing bugs, refactoring for performance, or migrating to a modern stack.
I'll review your codebase first and provide an honest assessment.
```

**Q4: Do you provide ongoing maintenance and support?**
```
Yes. I offer 30 days of post-launch support included with every project. After that,
I'm available for ongoing maintenance, feature additions, and performance monitoring
on a retainer basis. Your site won't be left abandoned.
```

**Q5: What's your development process like?**
```
I follow a collaborative approach: discovery call → proposal → design review →
development sprints → testing → launch. You'll have visibility into progress
throughout, and I'll keep communication open via your preferred channel.
